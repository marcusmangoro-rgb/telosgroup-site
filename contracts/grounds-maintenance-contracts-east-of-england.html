Grounds maintenance contracts coming up in the East of England

Councils, schools, housing associations and other public buyers in the East of England pay for grass cutting, parks, verges and grounds upkeep all the time. Most of that work runs on contracts of one to five years, and when a contract ends the buyer goes back to market. If you know what's ending, you can get ready, and get known to the buyer, before they ask for quotes.
