Building repairs and maintenance contracts coming up in the South East

Councils, schools, housing associations and other public buyers in the South East pay for responsive repairs, voids, planned maintenance and minor works all the time. Most of that work runs on contracts of a few years, and when a contract ends the buyer goes back to market. If you know what's ending, you can get ready, and get known to the buyer, before they ask for quotes.

How we counted

We count public contracts in this trade and area whose current term ends between November 2026 and May 2028, from published public contract records. The data is as of 23 November 2026. Each contract is counted once, even when it has several lots, suppliers or notices.

Where a contract's end date isn't published, we estimate it from its published start and length. Buyers can extend a contract or re-let it early, so treat the quarters as a guide, not a tender date.
