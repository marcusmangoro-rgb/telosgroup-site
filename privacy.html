Privacy and how we use your details

Last updated 23 October 2026

Who we are

Telos Group is run by Marcus Mangoro. Contact: marcus.mangoro@gmail.com.

Where we got your details

If we've emailed you without you asking, it's because your business won, bid for or was listed on a public contract, and the buyer published that award on the government's Find a Tender service or Contracts Finder. Those notices include the supplier's name and a business contact email. We also use Companies House records to check a business's name and address.

What we use them for

To tell you about public tenders and contracts that match your trade and area, and, if you ask, to send you a weekly list of them. We keep a short record of what you've told us you're interested in (for example the area you cover and the contract sizes you go for) so the list fits you.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: telling businesses about public contracts relevant to their work. We only email business addresses, never personal ones, and we don't email sole traders without their agreement.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your details.
  • We don't use tracking pixels or track whether you open our emails.
  • We don't add you to anyone else's list.

How long we keep them

For as long as you're receiving emails from us, and for up to 12 months after the last contact. If you ask us to stop, we keep only your email address on a "do not contact" list so we never email you again.

Stopping the emails

Reply to any email and say so, or email marcus.mangoro@gmail.com. We'll stop straight away.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, have them corrected or deleted, or object to how we use them. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Our email and data providers

Emails are sent through Google (Gmail) and IONOS. Tender data comes from Find a Tender and Contracts Finder and is used under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
