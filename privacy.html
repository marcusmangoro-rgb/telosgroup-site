Privacy and how we use your details

Last updated 16 November 2026

Who we are

Telos Group is run by Marcus Mangoro. Contact: mangoro@telosgroup.co.uk.

Where we got your details

If we've emailed you without you asking, it's because your business works in a trade we cover. We found it in publicly accessible sources: published public contract and spending records, and public registers of companies and businesses. The business contact email comes from a published public contract record or from your business's own website. We use public registers of companies to check a business's name, address and size, and sometimes take a director's name from them so we can address our email to that person.

What we use them for

To tell you about public tenders and contracts that match your trade and area, and, if you ask, to send you a weekly list of them. We keep a short record of what you've told us you're interested in (for example the area you cover and the contract sizes you go for) so the list fits you.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: telling businesses about public contracts relevant to their work. We only email business addresses, never personal ones, and we don't email sole traders without their agreement.

Reading your website

When a firm asks to be added to the Monday email, we may read its public website to set up its list: the services it offers, where it is based and the areas it covers. We only read pages anyone can see, and we tell you what we've set up so you can correct it.

Pages built from public contract records

Our "contracts coming up" pages are built from published public contract records. They show counts and value bands, and never name a buyer, a supplier or a person. If you think something on one of them is wrong, or you want it corrected or removed, email mangoro@telosgroup.co.uk and we'll put it right.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your details.
  • We don't use tracking pixels or track whether you open our emails.
  • We don't add you to anyone else's list.

How long we keep them

For as long as you're receiving emails from us, and for up to 12 months after the last contact. That includes anything we noted from your website. If you ask us to stop, we keep only your email address on a "do not contact" list so we never email you again.

Stopping the emails

Reply to any email and say so, or email mangoro@telosgroup.co.uk. We'll stop straight away.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, have them corrected or deleted, or object to how we use them. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Visits to this website

We count visits to this website with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and keeps nothing that identifies you. It shows us which pages are read and which sites visitors come from. It has nothing to do with our emails: we don't track whether you open them.
