Most small contracts in these trades never get advertised on Find a Tender

I looked at 1,427 contracts awarded under the Procurement Act between January and October 2026 in cleaning, grounds, waste, building maintenance and facilities management. Of the 659 worth under £140k, about 8 in 10 had no tender notice on Find a Tender at all, and only showed up once the contract had been awarded. A lot of that work goes to firms the buyer already knows, through invited quotes or frameworks.

Alert services only show you the work that gets advertised. So as well as the live tenders, each email tells you about a contract near you that's ending soon and who holds it now, which gives you the chance to get known to that buyer before they next ask for quotes.

Source: Telos Group analysis of Find a Tender award and tender notices, January to October 2026. Some of these contracts may have been advertised on Contracts Finder or elsewhere, so treat the figure as an estimate. Under £140k means below the central government services threshold of £139,688.
