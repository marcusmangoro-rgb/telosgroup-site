How it works

We watch every notice

Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, the government's own sites, checked every week. Nothing is behind a paywall; we just do the reading.

We match it to you

Your trade, how far you'll travel and the contract sizes you can take on. Two-stage tenders show the real first deadline, not the last one.

You get a short list

The best six, with a map and a link to each notice. Tell us what's useful and what isn't, and next week's list gets sharper.
