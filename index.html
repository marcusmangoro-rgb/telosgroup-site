Most small contracts in these trades weren't advertised on Find a Tender

We looked at 1,427 contracts awarded in these trades from January to October 2026. Of the 659 worth under £140k, about 8 in 10 had no tender notice on Find a Tender. They only showed up once the work had been awarded.

Alert services only show what's advertised. That's why each email also has a Coming up section, so you can get known to the buyer before they next ask for quotes.

Source: Telos Group analysis of Find a Tender award and tender notices, January to October 2026. Some of these contracts may have been advertised elsewhere, so treat the figure as an estimate. Under £140k means below the central government services threshold of £139,688.
