Most small public contracts are never advertised

We looked at 1,427 contracts awarded under the Procurement Act from January to October 2026 in cleaning, grounds, waste, building maintenance and facilities management. Of the 659 worth under £140k, about 8 in 10 had no tender notice at all. Buyers invited quotes from firms they already knew, or called off a framework, and only published the award afterwards.

Free alert services can't show you that work. What helps is knowing which buyers near you buy this way and when their contracts end, so you're on the list before they next ask for quotes. That's what the "Coming up" part of each email is for.

Source: Telos Group analysis of Find a Tender award and tender notices, January to October 2026. Under £140k means below the central government services threshold of £139,688.
